Against matched control stores. We measure:

  • Sessions that end in a purchase
  • 4-week repeat rate
  • Share of evening baskets

Risks we design for

  • Price claims get scrutinisedLabelled, audited delivery benchmarks only
  • Pushing junk foodCapped, clearly labelled brand slots
  • PrivacyWorks with mood and budget; history is opt-in
  • Unclaimed kitsPacked only after a tap; leftovers go to Reduced
What would prove us wrong

If fewer than 1 in 5 Tonight users come back within 4 weeks, the 5pm problem isn’t one Woolworths can own, and we stop.
